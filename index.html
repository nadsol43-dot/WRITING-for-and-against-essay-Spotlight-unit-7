Every story has two sides.

Learn to write a balanced for-and-against essay about online learning.

Start the lesson ↓Go to planningGo to writing
STEP 01 · 4 MIN

Online lessons: good or bad?

Today you will write a for-and-against essay about e-learning (online lessons).

Talk together:

1. Have you ever learned online? What was it like?

2. What is one good thing and one difficult thing about it?

Useful words: advantage = a good point · disadvantage = a bad point · e-learning = learning online
STEP 02 · 5 MIN

Read a short model

Read the essay. Find one advantage, one disadvantage and the writer’s opinion.

Online learning: advantages and disadvantages

Online learning is becoming more common. There are both advantages and disadvantages to studying online.

One advantage is that students can save time. For example, they do not need to travel to school. Some students can also watch recorded lessons again and learn at their own pace.

However, online learning also has disadvantages. Students may feel lonely if they study alone. In addition, internet problems can make them miss parts of a lesson.

All in all, online learning has both benefits and drawbacks. In my opinion, it is useful, but students also need chances to meet and learn with others.

Check together

Advantages: less travel and replaying recordings. Disadvantages: possible loneliness and internet problems. Opinion: online learning is useful, but students also need chances to meet others.

STEP 03 · 5 MIN

Understand the structure

Look at the model again. Find the topic sentence in each main-body paragraph.

1 · INTRODUCTION

Introduce the topic. Do not give your opinion yet.

2 · FOR

Give advantages. Support them with reasons or examples.

3 · AGAINST

Give disadvantages. Support them with reasons or examples.

4 · CONCLUSION

Summarise both sides and give a balanced opinion.

Topic sentence: One advantage is that students can save time.
Support: For example, they do not need to travel to school.
Find the other topic sentence

“However, online learning also has disadvantages.” The following sentences give examples of those disadvantages.

Use a formal, neutral style. Avoid slang and contractions such as “don’t”.

STEP 04 · 5 MIN

Match points and support

First say whether each point is for or against online learning. Then match 1–4 with A–D.

POINTS

1. Students can save travel time.

2. Students may feel lonely.

3. Recorded lessons can help students learn at their own pace.

4. Learning depends on technology.

REASONS / EXAMPLES

A. A poor internet connection can interrupt a lesson.

B. Students can replay a difficult explanation.

C. They do not need to travel to school.

D. They may have fewer chances to meet classmates in person.

Check the answers

1–C (for) · 2–D (against) · 3–B (for) · 4–A (against). Say one point and its support aloud.

STEP 05 · 5 MIN

Connect your ideas

Choose a linker from the box to complete each sentence. Then read it aloud.

LINKER BANK

One advantage is …   ·   For example, …   ·   However, …   ·   In addition, …   ·   All in all, …

1. Online lessons save travel time. __________, students can study from home.

2. Online learning has benefits. __________, it can also have drawbacks.

3. __________, online learning has both good and bad points.

Check the answers

1. For example   2. However   3. All in all

Notice the comma after the linking phrase.

STEP 06 · 4 MIN

Plan your essay

Your teacher has asked for an essay on the advantages and disadvantages of e-learning: 120–180 words.

Introduction: What is the topic?

For: One or two advantages → reason / example

Against: One or two disadvantages → reason / example

Conclusion: Summarise both sides → your opinion

Teacher: ask “Why?” or “Can you give an example?” for each point.

STEP 07 · 10 MIN

Write your first draft

Use your notes to write 120–180 words in four paragraphs. If you need more time, finish after class.

SENTENCE FRAMES

Online learning is … There are advantages and disadvantages.

One advantage is … For example, …
In addition, …

However, there are also disadvantages. One disadvantage is … For example, …

All in all, … In my opinion, …

Support your points with reasons or examples—not just a list.
